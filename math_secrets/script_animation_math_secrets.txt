What is the area of a triangle? keyframe_geometry_triangle_area.svg
freeze 30


caption-begin
So here's a simple way to draw a sketch that arrives at this formula, but without using words.  Sketch any triangle so the longest side of the triangle is at the bottom, meaning the bottom line is horizontal.  Then draw a horizontal line that goes through the top point of the rectangle.  Then draw two vertical lines that go through the points at each end of the bottom line.  You now have a rectangle that encloses the triangle.  To see how the area of the triangle compares to the area of the rectangle, draw a third vertical line that goes through the top triangle point.
