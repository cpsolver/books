One confusion about geometry is about the "proofs" that students are asked to understand, especially Euclid's proofs.  These proofs teach students what rigorous thinking looks like.  Math teachers hope students will be inspired to duplicate this careful thinking when, as an adult, they are bombarded with claims that cannot be supported with a carefully constructed argument.  The fact that geometric proofs are about lines and angles is less important.  Unlike what gets taught in English classes and debate classes, each step in a math proof requires absolutely no gaps in logic.
